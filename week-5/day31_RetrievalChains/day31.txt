Why Retrieval Chains?

Without Retrieval:

User Question
      ↓
LLM
      ↓
Hallucination Risk

Example:

What is our bank's home loan interest rate?

LLM doesn't know your company's internal policy.

With Retrieval:

User Question
      ↓
Retriever
      ↓
Relevant Documents
      ↓
Prompt
      ↓
LLM
      ↓
Answer

Now the answer comes from your documents.

Interview Questions
What is a Retrieval Chain?

A workflow that retrieves relevant documents before sending context to the LLM.

Why use Retrieval Chains?

Reduce hallucinations and provide factual answers.

What are the components of a Retrieval Chain?
Retriever
Prompt
LLM
Output
What is a Retriever?

Component that fetches relevant documents.

What is context injection?

Adding retrieved chunks into the prompt.

Difference between Chain and Retrieval Chain?

Chain:

Prompt → LLM

Retrieval Chain:

Retriever → Prompt → LLM
What is Top-K Retrieval?

Returning the most relevant K chunks.

Why use metadata filtering?

Improve retrieval precision.

What happens if retrieval returns irrelevant chunks?

Answer quality decreases.

How do you improve retrieval quality?
Better chunking
Better embeddings
Metadata filters
Re-ranking
Hybrid search
