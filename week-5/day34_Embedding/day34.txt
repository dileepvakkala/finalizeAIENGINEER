What is an Embedding?

An embedding is a numerical representation of text.

Example:

Home Loan

becomes

[0.23, 0.71, 0.14, ...]

Instead of words:

Interview Questions
What is an Embedding?

A dense numerical vector representing text meaning.

Why are embeddings used?

To perform semantic search and similarity matching.

What is vector search?

Finding vectors closest to a query vector.

What is cosine similarity?

A measure of similarity between vectors.

Why is cosine similarity used?

It efficiently measures semantic closeness.

What is dimensionality?

The number of values inside an embedding vector.

Difference between keyword search and vector search?
Keyword Search	Vector Search
Exact words	Semantic meaning
Limited	Intelligent
Synonym issues	Handles synonyms
What are Sentence Transformers?

Models used to generate embeddings locally.

What role do embeddings play in RAG?

They enable retrieval of relevant document chunks.

Why store embeddings in ChromaDB?

To perform fast similarity search.
