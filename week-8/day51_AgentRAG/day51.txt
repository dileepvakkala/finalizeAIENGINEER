Day 51 – Graph RAG Concepts (Knowledge Graph + RAG)

Today you'll learn one of the hottest enterprise AI topics:

Graph RAG (Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Traditional RAG retrieves chunks.

Graph RAG retrieves relationships between entities, making it far better for complex reasoning.

Companies using graph-based approaches include enterprise search, banking, healthcare, compliance, fraud detection, and knowledge management systems.

Why Traditional RAG Fails

Suppose your banking documents contain:

Customer A owns Account X

Account X transferred money to Account Y

Account Y belongs to Customer B

Customer B owns Company Z

Traditional RAG stores:

Chunk 1
Chunk 2
Chunk 3
Chunk 4

Query:

How is Customer A connected to Company Z?

Traditional retrieval may miss relationships.

Graph RAG Solution

Represent knowledge as a graph.

Customer A
    |
 owns
    |
Account X
    |
transferred
    |
Account Y
    |
belongs to
    |
Customer B
    |
owns
    |
Company Z

Now the system can traverse relationships.

Graph Traversal Example

Find all accounts owned by customer.

Pseudo-query:

MATCH (c:Customer)-[:OWNS]->(a:Account)
RETURN a

Find transaction chain:

MATCH p=(a)-[:TRANSFERRED_TO*]->(b)
RETURN p
Common Interview Questions
What is Graph RAG?

A RAG architecture that augments retrieval using knowledge graphs and entity relationships.

Why do we need Graph RAG?

Traditional RAG struggles with relationship-based queries and multi-hop reasoning.

What is a Knowledge Graph?

A graph of entities (nodes) and relationships (edges).

What are Nodes?
