What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing instructions that help LLMs produce accurate and reliable outputs.

Example

Bad Prompt:

Explain loans.

Good Prompt:

You are a banking expert.

Explain home loans in less than 100 words.

Include:
- Interest Rate
- Eligibility
- Repayment Period

Result:

More Accurate
More Structured
More Consistent

Why Few-Shot Works Better?

The model learns the expected pattern.

Example Banking Classification

Prompt:

Loan Approved → Positive

Loan Rejected → Negative

Loan Pending → Neutral

Classify:
Loan Under Review

Output:

Neutral
Session 2 (11 AM – 1 PM)
Chain of Thought (CoT)

Most important interview topic.

Instead of:

Give answer.

Ask:

Think step by step.

Example

Prompt:

A loan is 50 lakh.

Interest = 8%

Tenure = 20 years.

Calculate EMI step by step.

Benefits:

Better Reasoning
Higher Accuracy
Role Prompting

Give the model a role.

Example:

You are a senior banking consultant.

OR

You are a fraud detection expert.

Benefits:

Domain Expertise
Better Responses
Example
You are a tax consultant.

Explain tax benefits on home loans.

Prompt Templates

Very common in LangChain.

Template:

from langchain.prompts import PromptTemplate

template = """
You are a banking expert.

Question:
{question}

Answer:
"""

Usage:

prompt = template.format(
    question="What is EMI?"
)
System Prompt Design

Rules:

2. If information is unavailable say:

Interview Questions
What is Prompt Engineering?

The process of designing prompts that improve model performance.

What is Zero-Shot Prompting?

Giving instructions without examples.

What is Few-Shot Prompting?

Providing multiple examples before the task.

What is Chain of Thought?

Prompting the model to reason through steps before answering.

What is Role Prompting?

Assigning a specific role to the model.

Why use Delimiters?

To clearly separate instructions from data.

What is Structured Prompting?

Designing prompts that produce predictable formats such as JSON.

What is Prompt Injection?

An attack attempting to override system instructions.

How do you mitigate Prompt Injection?
Strong system prompts
Input validation
Context isolation
Output validation
Why are prompts important in RAG?

They guide how retrieved context is used.
