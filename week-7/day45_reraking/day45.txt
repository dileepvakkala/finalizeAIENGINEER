Interview Questions
What is Re-ranking?

A second-stage ranking process that improves retrieval quality.

Why use a Cross Encoder?

To obtain more accurate relevance scores between query and documents.

Difference between Bi-Encoder and Cross Encoder?

Bi-Encoder generates embeddings independently.

Cross Encoder evaluates query and document together.

What is Context Compression?

Removing irrelevant information before sending context to the LLM.

Why is Context Compression important?
Lower token cost
Better accuracy
Reduced noise
What is Lost in the Middle?

LLMs may ignore important information located in the middle of long contexts.

How do you solve Lost in the Middle?
Re-ranking
Context compression
Better chunk ordering
Where should Re-ranking happen?

After retrieval and before LLM generation.

Why not directly send all retrieved chunks?

Because irrelevant chunks reduce answer quality and increase cost.
