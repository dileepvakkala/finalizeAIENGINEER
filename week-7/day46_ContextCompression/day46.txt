Day 46 – Context Compression (Advanced RAG Optimization)

Today you'll learn one of the most important production RAG techniques.

Many RAG systems retrieve relevant documents but still fail because they send too much information to the LLM.

Interview Questions
What is Context Compression?

Reducing retrieved context while preserving information needed to answer a query.

Why is Context Compression important?
Lower cost
Lower latency
Better answer quality
What is Extractive Compression?

Selecting important sentences from retrieved documents.

What is Abstractive Compression?

Generating a concise summary of retrieved content.

Which is safer?
Extractive Compression

because it does not generate new information.

What is ContextualCompressionRetriever?

A LangChain retriever that compresses retrieved documents before passing them to the LLM.

How does compression reduce hallucinations?

It removes irrelevant context that can confuse the model.

What is Lost in the Middle?

Important information placed in the middle of long contexts may receive less attention from the LLM.

How do you optimize token usage in RAG?
Better chunking
Metadata filtering
Re-ranking
Context compression
When should compression happen?
After Retrieval

Before Generation
